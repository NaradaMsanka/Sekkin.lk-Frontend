# PayHere readiness audit — before implementation

Reviewed 9 November 2026. This is a local development audit, not PayHere approval or legal advice. Existing uncommitted storefront/cart/checkout improvements were present before this task.

PayHere's published review criteria require a complete site with actual goods, Terms, Returns and Privacy policies, and business contact information. Merchant approval is separately reviewed by PayHere's partner banks. About and Shipping pages, responsive design, and the detailed UX changes below support a clear customer experience; they are not separately listed as mandatory pages in the cited criteria. Sources: [Application process](https://support.payhere.lk/application-process), [Policy samples](https://support.payhere.lk/policy-samples).

## Local implementation boundary

Create six public pages and footer/checkout links, centralize confirmed business facts, mark missing decisions REVIEW REQUIRED, add draft metadata and a Vercel deployment check. No fabricated testimonials/products, legal windows, delivery dates or compliance certifications. No production writes, schema changes, push or deployment. Payment remediation is a separate pending scope decision; no live gateway integration in this task.

## Important limitations

Code inspection establishes the payment defects above; no real card details or live order submissions were used. Existing passwords/secrets were not copied into this report. Product images and real stock require read-only catalog checks and owner confirmation. Previous local screenshots are useful UI evidence, not proof of product authenticity. Readiness remains blocked until the mandatory facts, policies and critical security findings are resolved.
